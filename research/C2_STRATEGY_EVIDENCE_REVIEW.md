# C.2 Strategy and Mandate Evidence Review

**Review date:** 2026-11-23  
**Holdings transition under review:** 2025-09-30 to 2025-12-31  
**Cohort:** Six predeclared active-management funds  
**Evidence standard:** SEC prospectuses, prospectus supplements, shareholder reports, and contemporaneous fund disclosures. This is a documentary classification, not an independent audit of actual portfolio-manager behavior.

## Executive findings

The six funds are all actively managed, but the original growth/value/blend buckets are too coarse. The contemporaneous documents support two explicit growth mandates, two explicit value mandates, and two flexible growth/value mandates. Fidelity Magellan should not be described as a conventional blend fund: its prospectus says the adviser is not constrained by a particular investment style. Fidelity Contrafund also has explicit flexibility to invest in growth, value, or both.

Two important structural events need to accompany any interpretation of the 2025 transition:

1. **JPMorgan Large Cap Growth and JPMorgan Large Cap Value became non-diversified funds effective March 5, 2025.** This preceded the September–December observation window, so both endpoints in the C.2 transition occur after the change. It is not a within-window break, but it changes the legal concentration framework relative to earlier historical periods.
2. **Optimum Large Cap Growth's investment manager changed from Delaware Management Company to Nomura Investment Management Business Trust effective December 1, 2025.** The fund's two sub-advisers, American Century and Los Angeles Capital, were approved to continue under new sub-advisory agreements effective that date. This is a within-window manager/advisory-structure break. The reviewed filing documents continuity of the sub-advisers, but does not establish that asset allocations or day-to-day portfolio decisions were unchanged.

These findings do not invalidate the mechanical calculation. They do limit claims about stable management arrangements, strategy continuity, and attribution of the portfolio-change residual.

## Classification recommendation

Use the following descriptive groups in the cohort documentation:

- **Growth:** Optimum Large Cap Growth; JPMorgan Large Cap Growth.
- **Value:** JPMorgan Large Cap Value; Oakmark.
- **Flexible growth/value:** Fidelity Contrafund; Fidelity Magellan.

These are documentary strategy groupings, not Morningstar style-box classifications, and should not be used as a claim that each fund's realized portfolio exposures stayed inside a fixed style box during the quarter.

## Implications for interpreting the C.2 results

- Keep all six funds in the predeclared feasibility cohort. Do not silently reselect or remove a fund after seeing the results.
- Mark Optimum Large Cap Growth as having a within-window investment-manager/advisory-agreement transition on December 1, 2025. Its sub-advisers continued, but the available documents do not establish unchanged sub-adviser allocations or unchanged day-to-day decisions.
- Mark both JPMorgan funds as non-diversified from March 5, 2025. This is a pre-window structural change, not a September–December change.
- Do not call Contrafund or Magellan “blend” without qualification. “Flexible growth/value” is more faithful to their prospectuses.
- Keep the portfolio-change residual descriptive. It is not a causal estimate of manager trading and may reflect flows, corporate actions, manager/adviser changes, derivatives/cash, and reporting differences.
- The review does not satisfy a broader historical strategy-continuity audit. Before making longer-horizon comparisons, obtain earlier prospectuses and dated supplements for each fund and record manager/sub-adviser allocation changes.
